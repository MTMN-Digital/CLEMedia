## 1. The writing and production process

The review withdraws the six-stage account: "Our process involves repeated team
reviews, rather than one separate review assigned to each person. Alan leads
the writing, animation and creative direction throughout."

That account was on four pages and was the spine of two of them, so this is the
largest change in the pass. It is now the five phases the review sets out, in
its order and close to its words.

| | |
|---|---|
| **01 First draft** | Alan writes the script. Paula, Lydia, Kirstie, Conor and David Toth each read it and give feedback. |
| **02 Revised drafts** | Alan writes the next draft from that feedback and the same group reads it again. *Three to four rounds before the script is locked.* |
| **03 Animation** | Once the script is locked Alan begins the animation, using his creative judgement throughout production. |
| **04 Episode review** | The team watches the episode and gives feedback, and Alan makes the changes and shares the revised version. |
| **05 Final sign-off** | The team watches and revises again until everyone is happy and signs it off for release. *Repeats until everyone is happy.* |

Three things went with the old account, all named in the review:

- **"Signed off by" on each stage.** Nothing is signed off until the last phase.
- **"A release can be held here."** The red band that said so is gone. In its
  place, each repeating phase now says how many rounds it usually takes, which
  is the genuinely unusual thing about this process and had nowhere to be said
  before.
- **The portraits on each slip.** The same five faces on four of five slips is
  the third copy of the team's photographs on one page.

**David Toth is now in the reading group for every draft**, which the review
asks for by name. He was outside the sequence entirely before.

### What each page says now

**Home.** Heading was "A named person at every stage". Now **"Five people read
every draft"**, with: "Alan writes the scripts and animates the episodes.
Paula, Lydia, Kirstie, Conor and David read each draft and give him their
feedback, and he writes the next one from it."

**Team.** Heading was "Six stages, and who stands at each". Now **"How an
episode gets made"**. The page's second half asked "what each answers for" and
now asks "what each of them does".

**Responsible AI.** The drawn production line kept its shape and changed its
labels: five phases, the tools below the line at the one point they are used,
and the return path now reads "The episode goes back until everyone is happy"

**Our story.** "any of them can hold a release back" is now "the work goes
round between us until we are all happy with it".

## Addendum: the reading width, same day

Two further things were raised and both turned out to be one measurable fault
each rather than a matter of taste. Neither changed a word of the copy.

- **Pages were sitting in a narrow column in the middle of the screen.** The
  header and the footer run the full width of the page, starting their content
  32 pixels in. Several sections ran in a narrower box that was centred in the
  window instead, starting 240 pixels in, so the page appeared to step in and
  out as you scrolled. The founder's story did it five times down its length.
  Every section now starts where the header starts. Sections that want a
  narrower column still have one; it is simply an indent from the page's own
  edge rather than a separate box floating in the middle of it.
- **The team biographies were running 103 to 140 characters to the line.**
  Anything past about 75 is hard to read, and nothing else on the site was over
  80. They now run at the same width as the founder's letter, which makes them
  comfortable but taller, so each biography shows its opening paragraph and
  puts the career history behind a "More about" control. **Nothing was cut.**
  Every word the team supplied is still on the page, and the page is a third
  shorter.

The founder's story also gained a column down its right-hand side for the
drawings and the photographs of the team, which were previously squeezed into
the narrow notes column or pushed against the edge of the text.

## Addendum: the photographs of Finn and Fia, 26 October

The eight photographs arrived, and they are on the founder's story page now,
under "Meet the real Finn and Fia". **Item 1 of what CLE still needs to send is
closed.**

They are laid out as a gallery rather than as two plates. Each row of
photographs is sized so that every picture in it stands at the same height
while keeping its own shape, so the standing shots stay tall, the head shots
stay wide, and nothing is cropped square to fit a grid. On a phone they run one
under another at full width.

**One deliberate change to how they are labelled.** With two pictures, each one

three words eight times down one screen, which stops being a label and starts
being wallpaper. So the line above the gallery now says it in a sentence
instead, in bold, and each picture carries the dog's name. The description read
out to a blind reader also says "photographed" on every one. If you would
rather have the words under every picture, that is a one-line change.

The pictures are served at a web size and every piece of camera data is
stripped from them, including the timestamps, which is standard for anything we
put on a public site.
