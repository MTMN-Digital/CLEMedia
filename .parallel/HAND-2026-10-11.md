# The hand, as frozen on 2026-10-11

Read this before touching any drawing. It is the contract for this round and
it does not change while the round runs.

## What changed, and why you are being asked to redraw

The client's verdict, verbatim: "some drawings are still sub par ... and some
don't fully encapsulate what it is they are trying to."

He is right, and the cause was the same in every case: the drawings were
authored as **icon sets**. One uniform 2px stroke, perfect geometry, outline
only, no tone, no weight hierarchy, and a metaphor chosen because it was
drawable rather than because it was the thing the paragraph says.

Three things have already been done for you, centrally, and they are frozen:

1. **A wobble.** `components/draw/InkDefs.tsx` defines one SVG displacement
   filter and `.ink` applies it to every drawing on the site. Your paths stay
   exact in the source; the hand-drawn quality is added at paint time. Do not
   bake wobble into your coordinates, and do not add a filter of your own.
   `.ink--exact` on the svg is the opt-out, and it exists only for a drawing
   whose whole point is a ruled straight line.
2. **A wash.** `.ink-wash` and `.ink-wash--deep` are flat tone fills, drawn
   UNDER the line work. This is the single biggest lift available to you.
   Outline-only is why the old drawings read flat: every object was the same
   value, so nothing was in front of anything.
3. **A weight hierarchy.** `.ink-edge` (2.8) for an object's own silhouette,
   `.ink-line` (2) for ordinary line, `.ink-thin` (1.4, 55% alpha) for detail
   inside an object. **A drawing authored entirely in `.ink-line` has thrown
   this away and will still look like an icon whatever else you do to it.**

## The rules that did not change

- A drawing replaces a LAYOUT, never a sentence. **Nothing in the client's
  copy is cut to make room for one.** This is a standing instruction from the
  client and it is absolute.
- Draw the argument THIS page is making. If the drawing would fit another
  page, it is decoration and it is wrong.
- LEGIBLE BEATS UNIQUE. If a drawing needs a caption to be understood, it has
  failed. Two stages that do the same thing are allowed to look alike.
- Never an icon row. One drawing per idea, at scale.
- One accent per drawing, and it marks the single moment the drawing is about.
- It draws itself once and then stops. No loop, no drift, no parallax.
- `.ink-draw` requires `pathLength="1"` and CANNOT also carry a
  `stroke-dasharray` attribute: the class sets the dash array itself and the
  attribute is silently overridden.
- Every label is HTML beside the svg, never `<text>`, and is lifted from the
  page's own copy.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses to the finished pose. The finished pose
  is what a screenshot and a crawler see, so it has to be the right drawing.
- Contrast: `.ink-label` is `--color-body` on paper. `--color-muted` is a
  paper-only floor and fails on `.wall`, `.well`, `.bench` and `.deep`.
- No em dashes and no en dashes used as separators, anywhere, including
  comments.

## Files you may not touch

`src/components/draw/draw.css`, `src/components/draw/InkDefs.tsx`,
`src/index.css`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/components/Layout.tsx`, and any file
owned by another shard. If you believe you need a change in one of them, stop
and report it as a gap. Do not edit it.

## How to check your work

Do NOT run `npm run build`. Several shards are running at once and they would
write `dist/` over each other, which has previously produced a page of
contrast failures that did not exist.

Run `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.app.json` and read your own output. A
preview server is already running on port 4317 serving the PREVIOUS build, so
it will not show your change; do not be misled by it.
